ABOUT

Hey, I'm Juan Paolo Gines!

My dad has been a photographer for as long as I've known him, and he'd never let a moment or a memory pass without at least one photo to commemorate and look back on. It only took one gig for that same passion to ignite in me.

Through different settings, I've realized I find fulfilment in being the one responsible for making sure people have something to look back on. I've found that I thrive when capturing moments and emotions in their rawest form. I love that people can just be themselves knowing that I'll be there to document it.

Let's work together!
